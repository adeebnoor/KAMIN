قرار تموضع — سبتمبر 2026

كامن مكمّل للبنية الوطنية للمهارات، وليس بديلًا عنها.

هذا قرار معماري وتجاري: كامن لا يحاول بناء منصة وطنية موازية ولا يبقى منتجًا مغلقًا على جامعة واحدة. نقطة دخوله هي طبقة الأدلة الجامعية المملوكة للفرد، مع تصميم قابل للتوافق والتكامل مستقبلًا.

Positioning decision — September 2026

Kamin complements national skills infrastructure; it does not replace it.

This is both an architecture and market decision: Kamin does not attempt to build a parallel national platform and is not restricted to a single university. Its entry point is a learner-owned university evidence layer designed for future interoperability.

Decision: Complementary Evidence & Trust Layer

Kamin turns academic records and projects into explainable, portable capability evidence and connects that evidence to student, university, and employer decisions without claiming to be the national system of record.

We build

Portable individual evidence

Academic record + projects + consent + provenance + encrypted local backup.

We build

Explainable Fit

Why a pathway fits, what is missing, and the next step — without automated hiring decisions.

We build

University Evidence Adapter

A layer that connects course outcomes and evidence to updateable national/international capability taxonomies.

We do not build

A parallel national platform

Kamin does not duplicate national skills/training records or claim national-system authority.

We do not build

A KAU-only product

King Abdulaziz University can be an excellent pilot environment, but it is not the boundary of the product or data model.

We do not claim

Current government integration

No current API, endorsement, or government partnership is claimed. Any future connection requires an official channel.

Architecture implications

Canonical graphThe Person/Capability graph stays provider-neutral.
Crosswalk layerMappings to SASCED and other frameworks stay in a separate alignment layer rather than being hard-coded into product logic.
API boundaryFuture import/export and integrations go through explicit adapters with provenance and versioning.
Identity boundaryKamin does not require a national identity or central database to deliver its core learner value.
Portable evidenceThe encrypted local profile preserves learner ownership and makes future integration optional rather than mandatory.

Market path

Beachhead: final-year students and recent graduates through a defined university or program. The university is the first B2B2C channel, followed by Employer-Lite. If an official national integration path becomes available later, Kamin remains an evidence/explainability provider rather than a substitute for national infrastructure.
