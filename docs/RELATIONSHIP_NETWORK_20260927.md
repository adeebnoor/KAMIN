# Relationship network and student documentation

This release replaces the small radial landing illustration with a full-width typed relationship map. Four questions highlight paths through academic evidence, capabilities, occupations, interests, learning options and synthetic people/projects. Selecting a node shows adjacent relations; each selected path exposes source and scope. Arrows follow the predicate direction (a pathway requires a capability), even when the reading trail follows the connection in reverse.

The profile view projects supported semantic paths from the existing matching engine. Removing coursework removes academic connections; preferences do not become capability evidence. The map does not write claims, alter rankings or add people to personal profiles. Synthetic peer/project edges exist only in the standalone landing demo; real people matching and communication are not implemented. Advanced AI models remain planned work, clearly labelled on services.html and in the semester capstone roadmap.

The separate bilingual guide.html and services.html pages are linked in the primary navigation and sitemap. They explain actual workflows, persistence, withdrawal, source limits, local SPARQL scopes, and the distinction between working features, examples and planned services.

The roadmap now requires an end-of-semester v1, an approved three-college field pilot with disaggregated results, owned-domain deployment and evaluated AI features. These are future delivery gates, not claims of completed validation or a purchased domain.
